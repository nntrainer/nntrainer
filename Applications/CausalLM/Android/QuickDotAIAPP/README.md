# QuickDotAIAPP (Quick.AI)

The Android sample app that runs the QuickDotAI library: Chat, Metrics and
Power screens over the Gemma4 and LFM2 model packs on the Hexagon NPU, and
the CPU, QNN and LiteRT-LM engines where their packs are present.
