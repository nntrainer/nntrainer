Measuring the energy an on-device language model spends per generated token sounds simple and is not. The phone reports one current for the whole device, so the screen, the modem and the memory controller are inside every number, and the only way to attribute a part of it to the model is to measure the same phone twice: once idle with the model loaded, once while it answers, and subtract.

The battery gauge is a slow instrument. It integrates current over a window of a second or so and publishes a new reading when the window closes, which means a two-second prefill may be covered by two readings and a short answer by a handful. Long answers average well; short ones are noise. Sampling faster than the gauge updates only repeats the last value and should not be mistaken for resolution.
